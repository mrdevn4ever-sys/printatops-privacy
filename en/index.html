2. What stays on your device

The app stores the following only locally on your device. It is never sent to us and we cannot see it:

  • your saved favourites
  • the selected app language
  • the selected paper format
  • a counter for how often an ad has been shown
  • whether you have purchased the ad-free version

If you delete the app, this information goes with it.

3. What is sent to us

Feedback

If you write to us through the feedback form, we process:

  • the content of your message
  • the type of feedback (feedback or feature request)
  • the language set in the app
  • the processing status

What you type into the text field is up to you. We explicitly ask you not to enter names, addresses or email addresses — neither your own nor anyone else’s. A simple arithmetic question sits in front of the form so that children do not send something unintentionally.

Legal basis: your consent, given by submitting the form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You may withdraw it at any time by email; we will then delete your message as far as we can identify it.

Abuse protection

To stop the form from being abused at scale, we derive a cryptographic hash from your device’s IP address using a secret additional value, and store it together with a time window. We do not store the IP address itself. This hash is pseudonymous rather than anonymous, so we treat it as personal data.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a functioning service that resists abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Colouring pages and printing

When you print a colouring page, the app requests the corresponding print file from our server. What is transmitted is the identifier of the requested page, the paper format and the language setting. The server checks whether the page is published and returns a download link that expires after five minutes.

To protect against abuse we again derive a hash from your device’s IP address — as with the feedback form — and store it together with a time window. We do not store the IP address itself. Which page you print is not stored.

Legal basis: providing the function you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in a service that resists abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

No usage analytics

Earlier versions of the app recorded anonymised usage events. That feature has been removed entirely and the associated data has been deleted. Printatops keeps no statistics about which pages you view, print or save as favourites.

4. Advertising

Printatops shows ads from Google AdMob, usually after a successful print. You can buy the ad-free version once.

We have configured AdMob so that only non-personalised ads are served, the app is flagged as child-directed, and only ads rated G (suitable for all ages) are allowed. No advertising profile is built about you.

That does not mean no data is processed. According to Google, serving and accounting for ads involves in particular:

  • the IP address, among other things for approximate location
  • device and app identifiers
  • interactions with the ad, such as impressions and clicks
  • diagnostic and error data

Google is independently responsible for this processing. Details are in Google’s privacy policy.

Consent in the EEA, Switzerland and the United Kingdom

Before ads are loaded, the app shows you a dialog from Google that informs you about the data processing and lets you decide. Your choice is stored on the device. If you do not consent, limited ads are served without access to device identifiers. You can change your choice at any time in the app settings.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) where your device is accessed; otherwise our legitimate interest in funding the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Purchases

The one-time purchase of the ad-free version is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive neither your payment details nor your name — the app only learns whether a valid purchase exists. An arithmetic question sits in front of the purchase so that children cannot buy anything without a parent. Google Play’s terms and privacy policy apply to the payment itself.

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, as well as the right to object to processing. Where you have given consent, you may withdraw it at any time with effect for the future.

One limitation you should know about: because Printatops works without an account, we cannot technically identify you. For feedback messages we can only make a match if you give us enough to go on, such as the approximate time and the content. In such cases we do what we can.

For any request, an email to puredevn.io@gmail.com is enough.

9. Children

Printatops is aimed at children and at adults who colour with them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

For users in the United States, the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) applies. We do not use personalised advertising and we do not share children’s data with third parties for advertising purposes. The feedback form is protected by an arithmetic question so that it is not operated by a child alone.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that information about your child has nevertheless reached us, write to us — we will delete it.
